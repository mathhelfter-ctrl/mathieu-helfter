About me

I am a post-doctoral researcher at ITSA with Vadim Kaloshin since October 2024. Beforehand I completed my Phd thesis on "Complexity and bifurcation of the identity" under the supervision of Pierre Berger at IMJ-PRG, Sorbonne Université and CNRS. I am interested various subjects of dynamical systems and probabilities, most importantly (multi) fractals in infinite dimensions, spectral rigidity of standard and billard maps, ergodicity, renormalization of smooth diffeomorphism, geometric measure theory.

A short explanation

Set

\[ V_{K,\varepsilon}(x) = -\frac{K}{2\pi}\cos(2\pi x) -\frac{\varepsilon}{10\pi}\cos(10\pi x), \qquad \partial_xV_{K,\varepsilon}(x) = K\sin(2\pi x)+\varepsilon\sin(10\pi x). \]

The parameters \(K\) and \(\varepsilon\) vary the two Fourier modes of this potential. The standard map is

\[ y' = y+\partial_xV_{K,\varepsilon}(x), \qquad x'=x+y'\pmod 1. \]

For a sampled rational rotation number \(\omega=p/q\), the plotted finite-period approximation is

\[ \beta_{K,\varepsilon}^{[q]}\!\left(\frac pq\right) =\frac1q\min_{x_q=x_0+p}\sum_{n=0}^{q-1} \left[ \frac12(x_{n+1}-x_n)^2 -\frac{K}{2\pi}\cos(2\pi x_n) -\frac{\varepsilon}{10\pi}\cos(10\pi x_n) \right]. \]

The dashed curve is the \(V=0\) case, namely \(K=\varepsilon=0\), for which \(\beta_0(\omega)=\tfrac12\omega^2\).

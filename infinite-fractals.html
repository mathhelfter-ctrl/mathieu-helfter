The model behind the display

For \(\gamma>0\), let

\[ K_\gamma=\{(n^{-\gamma}\varepsilon_n)_n:\varepsilon_n\in\{0,1\}\}\subset c_0, \]

with the supremum norm. At depth \(N\), there are \(2^N\) cylinders at radius \(r_N=(N+1)^{-\gamma}\); consequently, the corresponding power-law dimension grows without bound.

For the product measure \(\mu_p\), the displayed spectrum is exact for this model:

\[ \alpha_p(q)=-q\log p-(1-q)\log(1-p), \qquad f_p(\alpha_p(q))=-q\log q-(1-q)\log(1-q). \]

This is a model-specific description, not a universal representation of infinite-dimensional fractals.

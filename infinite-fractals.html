The model behind the display

For γ > 0, let Kγ = {(n-γεn)n : εn ∈ {0, 1}} in c0, with the supremum norm. At depth N, there are 2N cylinders at radius rN = (N + 1)-γ; consequently, the corresponding power-law dimension grows without bound.

For the product measure μp, the displayed spectrum is exact for this model: αp(q) = -q log p - (1 - q) log(1 - p), while fp(αp(q)) is the binary entropy of q. This is a model-specific description, not a universal representation of infinite-dimensional fractals.
